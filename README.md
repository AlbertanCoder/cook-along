# Shad's Cook Along

**Open it here (best on a phone): https://albertancoder.github.io/cook-along/**

A step-by-step cooking app for your phone. Pick a meal and you get one step at a time, in big text, with a Next
button at the bottom of the screen.

- **One step at a time.** Tap Next or swipe. The screen stays on while a recipe is open.
- **Timers.** Start one from any step. It floats at the top of the screen and beeps when it's done, even if you've
  moved on.
- **Amounts that fit what you bought.** Type in how much meat you actually have and every amount in the recipe
  updates.
- **Plain words.** Tap any underlined word in a step (simmer, dice, baste) for a short explanation.
- **Week & groceries.** Pick a meal for each day of the week, then tap a day to see just what to buy for it, or see
  the whole week in one list.
- **Leftovers.** The fridge list tracks what's in your fridge and freezer and how many days each thing has left.
- **Notes.** Rate each meal and write what you liked and what you'd change, then copy your notes in one tap.

To add it to an iPhone home screen: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

The meals are mostly healthy, cheap rice bowls: chicken burrito, beef taco, chicken fajita, chicken tinga and street
corn bowls, plus chili, picadillo, salmon and leftover fried rice.

Recipes based on RecipeTin Eats (chili, salmon), Cooking Classy (burrito bowls) and Laura in the Kitchen
(picadillo), adjusted for less salt and no sugar. The taco, fajita, tinga and street corn bowls were written for
this app from the classic versions of each dish.

Everything is in one file, `index.html`. Your progress is saved on your own phone, not online.
